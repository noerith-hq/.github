# NOERITH

> Building open-source data systems and applied AI to turn complex information into reliable intelligence.

NOERITH is an independent lab focused on practical foundations for data, knowledge, and agentic software.

## What we build

- Data and AI platforms that make complex information usable.
- Ontology-driven systems, knowledge graphs, and semantic interfaces.
- Agentic workflows with traceable context and human control.
- Open-source tools and reusable engineering foundations.

## Vision

Make complex information understandable, operable, and trustworthy.

## Principles

- **Evidence before assertion** — make important decisions traceable to reliable sources.
- **Secure by default** — protect data, identities, dependencies, and runtime access.
- **Interoperable by design** — prefer clear contracts, open standards, and composable systems.
- **Human responsibility** — keep meaningful review and control in the loop.
- **Useful openness** — share practical knowledge, reusable tools, and reproducible results.
