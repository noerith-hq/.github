# Code of Conduct

NOERITH is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Give and receive feedback about ideas and code, not people.
- Explain disagreement with evidence and keep discussions solution-oriented.
- Protect confidential information and respect project boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may clarify, edit, remove, or decline contributions that violate this code. Report conduct concerns privately to the organization owner through GitHub. Use the Security Policy for vulnerability reports.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a good-faith report is not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to project spaces, issues, pull requests, discussions, events, and other interactions representing NOERITH.
